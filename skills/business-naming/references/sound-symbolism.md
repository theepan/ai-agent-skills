# Sound Symbolism Reference

Use letter and sound associations to steer the **feel** of a name. Based on
Lexicon Branding's linguistic engine and phonosemantics research.

## How to Use

1. **During Invent** -- After defining behavior in Phase 1, select 2-3 target
   sounds that match. Use them as a lens when generating and filtering.
2. **During Filter** -- Check shortlisted names against target sounds. Names
   whose dominant sounds conflict with the desired behavior should be
   reconsidered.
3. **As a tiebreaker** -- When two names are equally strong on meaning, the
   one whose sound better matches the desired behavior wins.

Do not force it. Sound symbolism is a lens, not a rule. A great name with
"wrong" sounds still beats a mediocre name with "right" sounds.

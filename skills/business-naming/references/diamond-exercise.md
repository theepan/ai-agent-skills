# Diamond Exercise

A startup-oriented brief template. Use when the user has limited context or
is an early-stage company. Replaces generic mission statements with a
structured framework for what the name must achieve.

## The Diamond

```
              TOP: WIN
       What does winning look like
            for the company?

LEFT: WHAT YOU                      RIGHT: WHAT YOU
   HAVE TO SAY                      HAVE TO WIN
What must we communicate           Current assets and
to the market to win?               strengths.

              BOTTOM: WHAT YOU NEED TO WIN
           Gaps and resources you need.
```

## How to Fill It

### 1. Win (Top)

Describe the desired end state. Be specific about outcomes, not vague
aspirations.

- Bad: "Be the best in our category"
- Good: "Trades professionals complete 30% more jobs per week because admin
  is automated"

### 2. What You Have to Win (Right)

List existing assets, strengths, and advantages. What is already in place?

- Team expertise, technology, partnerships
- Existing users or traction
- Unique insight or IP

### 3. What You Need to Win (Bottom)

List gaps, missing resources, and dependencies. What is not yet in place?

- Market awareness, trust, distribution
- Funding, team members, partnerships
- Product capabilities not yet built

### 4. What You Have to Say (Left)

The one thing the market must hear to choose you. This is the core message,
not a tagline -- the underlying truth.

- Bad: "We're innovative and customer-focused"
- Good: "You'll see it clearly" / "Your day runs itself"

## Why It Works

Mission statements are too abstract to guide naming. The Diamond forces
specificity: what you want, what you have, what you lack, and what you must
communicate. This produces a brief grounded in real strategic position, not
aspirational fluff.

## Example: Real Estate Website

| Corner | Answer |
|--------|--------|
| **Win** | People find or sell with confidence; the site is the clear path |
| **What you have to win** | Strong UX, listings, credibility |
| **What you need to win** | Trust, traffic, standing out from big players |
| **What you have to say** | "You'll see it clearly" |

## Example: Skilled Trades Automation

| Corner | Answer |
|--------|--------|
| **Win** | Trades pros complete more jobs; admin runs itself |
| **What you have to win** | Deep trades knowledge, working automation engine |
| **What you need to win** | Market trust, distribution, brand awareness |
| **What you have to say** | "Your day runs on beat" |
